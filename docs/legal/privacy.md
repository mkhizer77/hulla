# Hulla privacy policy

> Draft for legal review (#87). Square brackets mark facts only the owner can fill in. Not published yet. Every statement here is checked against the code; `docs/legal/data-inventory.md` names the file behind each one.

Last updated: [date of publication]

Hulla is a card game app for playing with friends. This policy says what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. [Operator's legal name] ("we") is the controller of your data. Contact: [support address], [postal address].

## The short version

- You can play without giving us your name, email or phone number.
- We don't sell your data and we don't show you ads based on tracking unless you allow it.
- Voice chat is live and never recorded.
- You can download or delete everything from Settings.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Legal basis (UK and EU) |
|---|---|---|
| **Account:** a random id made on your phone, your display name, handle, bio, picture, frame and title, your friend code, and your game stats | To give you an account and show you to other players | Contract |
| **Birth year**, which you give once when you start | To keep under-13s out, and to apply the limits for younger players (no voice or free text with strangers under 16, no personalised ads under 18) | Legal obligation |
| **Friends:** your friends, favourites, recent players, mutes and blocks | Social features you ask for | Contract |
| **Online status:** whether you're online, at a table or away, and when you were last seen (you can hide it) | To show your friends when you can play | Contract |
| **Sign-in links**, if you choose them: the id that Google, Facebook or Apple gives us for you | So you can sign in on another phone | Contract |
| **Phone number**, if you link one | So you can sign in with a code. We keep only a one-way hash of the number, never the number itself | Contract |
| **Contacts and Facebook friends**, only if you tap Find friends and allow it | To find which of your contacts play Hulla. Numbers are hashed and matched, then thrown away; we don't store your contacts. If you link Facebook, friends who also play are added to your friends list | Consent |
| **Games:** the cards dealt, every move and its time, and the results | To run the game, to let anyone check a deal was fair, to settle disputes and to catch cheating | Contract and legitimate interest |
| **Text chat** at a table | To deliver it to the table, and so a report can include it | Contract and legitimate interest (safety) |
| **Voice chat** | Sent live to the other players at your table only. **Never recorded or stored** | Contract |
| **Purchases:** the store's transaction id and what you bought | To give you what you paid for and restore it | Contract |
| **Reports and moderation:** reports you make or that name you, with the table's chat from the last 7 days and the reported player's profile; warnings, mutes and bans | To keep players safe | Legitimate interest and legal obligation |
| **Push notifications:** a device token, your language and time zone | To send the notifications you allow, at sensible hours | Consent (your phone's setting) |
| **Usage and crash data**, only if you say yes to "Share anonymous usage data": counts and timings such as round length, which screen you opened and crash reports. No name, chat or cards | To fix problems and improve the game | Consent |
| **Advertising:** your phone's advertising id and ad interactions, collected by our ad partner | To show ads and reward you for watching one. Personalised only if you allow it, and never under 18 | Consent (personalised), legitimate interest (contextual) |
| **IP address** | Seen by our servers for every connection; stored only as a short-lived counter to stop phone-code abuse | Legitimate interest |
| **Look feedback**, if you answer: a rating and an optional comment about a table theme | To decide which look to keep | Consent |

We don't collect your precise location, your photos or your exact date of birth.

## Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run parts of Hulla for us, under contracts that limit what they can do with it:

| Service | What it does | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| [Hosting provider and region] | Runs our servers and database | Everything above that we store |
| Cloudflare | Protects the servers and hosts our link pages | Connection data such as your IP address |
| Google Firebase | Push notifications; usage and crash reports if you said yes | Device token; usage events and crash reports |
| AppLovin (MAX) and its ad networks | Ads | Advertising id, device data, ad interactions, and your Hulla id so a rewarded ad pays the right account |
| Meta (WhatsApp) and Twilio | Sending sign-in codes | Your phone number and the code |
| Google, Facebook, Apple | Sign-in, if you use it | What you agree to on their screen |
| Google Play and the App Store | Payments | Payment details stay with them; we get a transaction id |

Players see your display name, handle, picture, bio, level, title and stats, and your online status unless you hide it. Players at your table hear your voice and see your messages.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. We never sell personal data.

## Children

Hulla is for players 13 and over. When you start, we ask your birth year. If you're under 13 you can't play. If you're under 18, you get no personalised ads; under 16, you can't use voice or free text with players who aren't your friends. If you believe a child under 13 is using Hulla, tell us at [support address] and we'll delete the account.

## Your rights

You can see, download and delete your data in Settings > Account (Export my data, Delete account). Without the app, request deletion at [link domain]/delete or by writing to [support address] from the phone number or account you used. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to correct your data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent at any time (Settings > Privacy) and complain to a data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK. In California you can ask what we collect and ask us not to sell or share it; we don't sell it, and turning off personalised ads stops sharing for ads.

## Security

Connections are encrypted. Phone numbers and sign-in codes are stored only as hashes. Access to the servers is limited to the people who run Hulla.

We'll tell you in the app before an important change to this policy takes effect.
