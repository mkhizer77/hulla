# Basant: the tab screens, the sheets and the rest

Built 28 Oct 2026 to the approved concepts of 27 Oct.

## Frame

Friends, Trophies, Shop, Profile, Settings, Ranked, the rulebook and the phone pages share the list frame: the calm sky, the title in the lettered face, round icon buttons in the top bar (back, done, add), and a scroll of cream cards. Under Trophies a segmented control (`SegmentBar`) switches Missions, Achievements and Leaderboard; under Shop it switches Shop and Pass. The Trophies tab opens on Missions.
