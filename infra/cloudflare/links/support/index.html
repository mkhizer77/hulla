Help and support

Email hulla.games1@gmail.com. Put your friend code in the message (Profile in the app) and tell us what happened. We reply within 14 days.

Common questions

  • Report a player: tap their picture at the table and choose Report. The table's chat goes with the report.
  • A purchase didn't arrive: go to Settings > Account and data > Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the order number from Google Play or the App Store.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account.
