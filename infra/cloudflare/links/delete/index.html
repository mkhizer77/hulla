Delete your Hulla account

In the app

  1. Open Hulla and go to Settings > Account and data.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

You have 14 days to change your mind: tap Cancel deletion on the same screen. After 14 days your profile, friends, progress, coins, items and messages are erased for good. You can download your data first with Export my data.

Without the app

Email hulla.games1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hulla account". Send it from the email or Google, Facebook or Apple account you signed in with, or tell us the phone number you linked and your friend code, so we can find the account. We reply within 14 days and confirm when it is deleted.

What we keep

Reports and moderation decisions are kept for a year after the report, and bans until they end, so deleting an account can't be used to dodge a ban. Details are in the privacy policy.
