Thulla night, wherever your friends are.

Hulla brings the card games you play at home, at college and on the rooftop to your phone. The first game is Thulla, also called Bhabhi: everyone races to empty their hand, everyone must follow the suit, and the player who can't throws a thulla that sends the whole pile to someone else. The last one holding cards is the Bhabhi.

PLAY WITH YOUR OWN PEOPLE
• Make a private room and send the code on WhatsApp. Friends tap the link and sit down.
• 3 to 6 players at a table.
• Talk while you play with voice chat in the room, or use quick chat and emotes.
• Watch a friend's table; the host can seat you for the next round.

YOUR HOUSE RULES
• Standard, Family, Hardcore, Classic Pagat and Relaxed rule sets.
• The host can change hand-taking, the endgame, the timer and what the loser is called.
• The rulebook is always one tap away at the table.

PLAY ANY TIME
• Play now finds you a table in seconds.
• Ranked seasons with leagues from Bronze to Ustad.
• Daily missions, a daily reward, weekly leaderboards and achievements.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
• Every deal is shuffled on the server and can be checked after the round.
• Block, mute and report from any player's card. Chat filters for English, Urdu, Hindi and Bengali.
• No betting and no real money: coins are only for cosmetics.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Card backs, tables, frames, emotes and sound sets.
• A season pass with a free track.

FOR EVERYONE
• A four-colour deck, high contrast, larger text and cards, a left-handed layout, reduce motion and screen reader support.
• A short tutorial teaches the game in a few hands.
